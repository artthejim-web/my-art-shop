About Me

Hello😉 Welcome to my world♥️ I'm Jim and since this is the about section I should tell you about me. I am an artist. Charcoal, graphite, colored pencils, pastels are my favorites. I will not bore you with my family's history but simply put, my dad's an artist and my late grandfather was a photographer. That explains my specialties. Drawing and taking and editing pictures is like a drug for me. I can't imagine myself without them. This is what I love doing and I do it well. I'm hoping that by reading this you will help me do what I love doing. And that's my about section. I intend to form a meaningful relationship with YOU as the reader.🫂
