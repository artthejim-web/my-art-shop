About Me

Hello😉 Welcome to my world♥️ I'm Jim and since this is the about section I should tell you about me. I am an artist. Charcoal, graphite, colored pencils, pastels and mixed media are my chosen tools. My art explores human emotion, depth and connection through portraiture and figurative work. Each piece tells a story and invites you into my perspective of the world. Whether through commissioned portraits or gallery pieces, I aim to create work that resonates and moves the viewer. Thank you for supporting my art!
